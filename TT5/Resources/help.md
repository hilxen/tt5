**A Text to Speech App for Twitch**

**Reads comments posted to a channel.**

The app is useful for both channel owners and viewers, so they do not need to read comments from the screen, but instead use an earpiece or speaker.

The app can be put into the background, so you can run other streaming apps like PRISM Live at the same time, or simply put your phone in a pocket.

**How to put the app into background:** The app automatically starts stream preview in picture-in-picture mode. This puts the app into the background! Now you can open other apps or lock your phone. Tap on refresh button to restart stream preview

**Tip:** If you are a streamer, you can prevent hearing yourself by increasing speech volume in settings or keeping the stream preview on mute.

**Features**

- No login required
- Twitch channel view and reading comments is anonymous
- The app does not collect any data
- Background mode
- Languages and voices - all supported by Apple's Text to Speech
- Automatic language recognition
- Customize speech at app level or for individual nicks - Voice, Volume, Rate, Pitch
- Mute/unmute nicks by role or individually, or for highlighed text, or custom rewards
- Customize nick's pronunciation
- Announce custom rewards

**New in version 1.4**
- announce custom rewards with alert sound
- custom reward MUST have text
- Twitch does not provide custom reward names to anonymous users, so you have to set it yourself:
  > tap on custom reward message to set name and alert sound
  > configure custom reward name and alert sound in settings
